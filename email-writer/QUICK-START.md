# Email Writer · what to say

Say these in plain words. You don't need the exact phrase. The slash command works too.

| Say | It does | Skill |
|---|---|---|
| set up my emails | Makes your Email folder, fills your Email Brain, imports your Email Room download | `/email-writer:email-setup` |
| add an idea: my client sent receipts in a shoebox | Saves the idea with the date and the best email type | `/email-writer:email-idea` |
| find me ideas | Reads your ideas, recent notes and calendar (if connected) and suggests 10 | `/email-writer:email-idea` |
| write next month's emails | Plans 4 weeks, writes about 10 emails, rates each one, saves a calendar, opens the preview | `/email-writer:email-month` |
| write a client win | One email from your proof bank (asks before naming anyone) | `/email-writer:email-write` |
| write a story email / a 9-word email / a how-to / an event invite | One email (an event invite is three) | `/email-writer:email-write` |
| fix my email | Editor mode for your own draft: three kind notes, the polished version, three subject lines | `/email-writer:email-fix` |
| help me write the 8-minute email | Coaches the 8-minute habit, then edits it | `/email-writer:email-fix` |
| give me subject lines for this | 10 options, coloured counts, one pick | `/email-writer:email-subjects` |
| rate my email / make it a 10 | A separate rater scores it, then it gets rewritten from the fixes, up to 3 rounds | `/email-writer:email-rate` |
| show me my emails | Opens the inbox preview with copy buttons | `/email-writer:email-preview` |
| help me load these into GoHighLevel (or Mailchimp, ActiveCampaign) | Step-by-step paste and schedule guide | `/email-writer:email-paste` |
| Friday scoreboard | Logs sent, replies, new subscribers, calls booked, and gives one upgrade | `/email-writer:email-scoreboard` |

Add **quick draft** to any writing request to skip the rating.

## The weekly habit

- **Monday:** "find me ideas", then "add an idea: ..." for anything from your phone photos.
- **When you sit down to write:** "write next month's emails" once a month, or one email at a time.
- **Before you send:** fill the brackets, read it out loud, "help me load these into ...".
- **Friday:** "Friday scoreboard".

Nothing is ever sent for you.
