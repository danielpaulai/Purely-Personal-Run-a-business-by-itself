# Finding Ideas

You never run out of ideas. They are already piling up. You just have to look.

## The 9 places ideas pile up

Check these once a week, on Monday.

1. **Your phone photos and screenshots.** The best place of all.
2. **Your calendar.** Every call and meeting from last week.
3. **Your chats.** WhatsApp, DMs, group chats.
4. **Your clients.** Questions they asked, wins they had, where they got stuck.
5. **Your community.** What people asked in groups and comments.
6. **What you're making.** A workshop, a post, a new offer.
7. **What you're reading and watching.** A film, a book, a podcast.
8. **What happened this week.** A trip, a meal, a mistake, a surprise.
9. **What you spent money on.** Why you bought it says a lot about buying.

## The link

Something you saw + your business = an email.

A photo of a crowded cafe + your business = "Why the busiest cafe on my street never runs ads."

## The 60-second drill

Set a timer for 60 seconds each and write three lessons for your clients from:
- your favourite film
- a child (yours, a niece, a kid you saw at the shop)
- something that happened to you this week

That's nine ideas in three minutes.

## Catch ideas on the go

When an idea hits and you're out, email yourself with **IDEA** in the subject line. On Monday, look through them. With the plugin, say "add an idea: …" and it's saved for you.

## Ideas go stale

The feeling fades fast. Write the email while you still care about the idea, ideally the same day or the next.
