# Ten Email Words

1. **List:** the people who said yes to your emails.
2. **Subscriber:** one person on your list.
3. **Broadcast:** one email sent to your whole list (or a tag) at once.
4. **Subject line:** the bold line people see in their inbox.
5. **Preview text:** the grey line after the subject.
6. **Tag:** a label on a contact, like "newsletter" or "ask first", so you can email the right people.
7. **Signup form:** the box where people type their email to join your list.
8. **Welcome email:** the email that goes out by itself when someone joins.
9. **Unsubscribe:** the link at the bottom so people can leave. Your tool adds it for you.
10. **Sending domain:** your own web address (like yourname.com) that your emails come from, so they don't land in spam.
