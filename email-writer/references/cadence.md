# The Weekly Cadence

Pick how many emails a week you can promise for the next 30 days. One is fine.

| Day | Email | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | Plan + 9-Word | Look for ideas, plan the week. Every 2 to 3 weeks, send a 9-word email. |
| Tuesday | How-To | Teach one useful thing. Three tips. |
| Wednesday | Win Wednesday | A client win. Before, method, after. |
| Thursday | Story | Something real happened. The lesson. One next step. |
| Friday | The Friday Upgrade | This week's win, the lesson, and one upgrade for next week. |

## By how many you promise

- **1 a week:** Wednesday. Rotate: Client Win, How-To, Story, 9-Word.
- **2 a week:** Tuesday How-To, Thursday Story or Client Win.
- **3 a week:** Tuesday How-To, Wednesday Win, Friday Upgrade.
- **5 a week:** the full table.

## Campaign weeks

When you are filling a workshop or opening your program, swap one slot for the Event Invite emails. About two weeks a month, no more.

## Friday scoreboard

Write down four numbers: emails sent, replies, new subscribers, calls booked. Missed a week? Just send the next one.
