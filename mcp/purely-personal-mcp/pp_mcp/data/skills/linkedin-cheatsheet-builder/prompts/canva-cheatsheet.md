Create a premium, professionally designed cheatsheet graphic based on the following copy.

STYLE & QUALITY:
The design should feel like it was created by a world-class editorial and brand designer. Modern, minimal, premium, clean, highly readable, visually balanced. Avoid generic Canva templates. The final result should feel polished enough for a high-end business, creator brand, or tech company.

BRAND COLOURS (Purely Personal):
- Primary red: #e90d41 (use for accents, key numbers, the title underline, and the bottom CTA strip only, not for body text)
- Off-white background: #f7f7f8
- Near-black text: #0a0a0a
- Subtle paper-grid texture in the background, very low opacity

TYPOGRAPHY:
- Headings: Rethink Sans or a similar geometric sans, tight tracking
- Body: Inter or a similar humanist sans, generous line height
- Numbers and labels: JetBrains Mono or a similar mono for accent

DESIGN DIRECTION:
- Elegant grid-based layout
- Strong typography hierarchy, the title at the top should be 3-4x the body size
- Sophisticated spacing and alignment, generous margins
- Minimal but impactful, no clutter
- Editorial-style composition, like a New York Times info-card
- Visually engaging without being decorative for its own sake

LAYOUT REQUIREMENTS:
- Strong title at the top, with a 4px red underline (#e90d41)
- Organise the content into clear numbered sections, cards, or blocks
- Use visual hierarchy to guide the eye top-to-bottom
- Add elegant dividers and subtle geometric shapes where appropriate
- Optimised for social media, vertical 1080x1350 format
- Margins and spacing extremely polished

ICONS & ILLUSTRATIONS:
- Custom-style line icons next to each numbered item
- Icons should feel cohesive, premium, modern, thin-line style
- One consistent illustration style throughout
- Blend visuals naturally into the composition, no random decoration

TEXT STYLING:
- Important keywords in bold, never in colour except the primary red accent
- Varying font sizes for hierarchy
- Excellent readability on mobile
- Avoid overcrowding text, leave breathing room

OUTPUT STYLE:
The final design should feel like a mix of:
- Apple-style minimalism
- Modern startup brand book
- Premium editorial infographic (think Monocle, The Economist briefings)
- High-end LinkedIn save-worthy carousel
- Luxury productivity brand aesthetic

OPTIONAL ENHANCEMENTS:
- Soft shadows under cards
- Subtle texture overlays on the background
- Thin line illustrations
- Premium UI-inspired elements (corner brackets, fine rules)

FORMAT:
- Vertical, 1080x1350 (LinkedIn-optimal)
- High resolution
- Crisp typography
- Perfect alignment and spacing

BOTTOM CTA STRIP:
- Add a small red strip at the bottom (#e90d41) with the author's name + handle in white text
- Keep the strip thin (8-12% of total height) so it does not dominate

## Tips for the best output

1. **Run the prompt as-is the first time.** Canva AI gets it right ~70% of the time on the first pass.
2. **If the design is too busy:** ask Canva "make it more minimal, remove decorative elements, more whitespace."
3. **If the red is overpowering:** ask Canva "reduce the red to accents only, keep body text near-black."
4. **If icons are missing:** ask Canva "add thin-line icons next to each numbered item, consistent style."
5. **Save as a Canva template** the first time. Every future cheatsheet uses the same template, just swap the copy.
