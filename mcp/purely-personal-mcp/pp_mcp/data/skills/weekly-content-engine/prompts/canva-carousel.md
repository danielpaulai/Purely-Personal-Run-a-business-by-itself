---

Create a premium, professionally designed LinkedIn carousel based on the following slide content.

STYLE & QUALITY:
The design should feel like it was created by a world-class brand designer for a top creator. Modern, minimal, premium, clean, high-contrast, swipe-friendly. Avoid generic Canva templates. Each slide should make the reader want to swipe to the next.

BRAND COLOURS (Purely Personal):
- Primary red: #e90d41 (use for cover slide accent, slide number badges, CTA slide background, and the underline on slide titles, not for body text)
- Off-white background: #f7f7f8 (slides 2 to N-1, body slides)
- Near-black background: #0a0a0a (slide 1 cover + slide N CTA, for visual bookending)
- Off-white text on dark slides, near-black text on light slides
- Subtle paper-grid texture on light slides, very low opacity

TYPOGRAPHY:
- Slide titles: Rethink Sans or similar geometric sans, large (60-80pt), tight tracking
- Slide body: Inter or similar humanist sans, medium (28-32pt), generous line height
- Slide numbers + labels: JetBrains Mono or similar mono, small (16-20pt)

LAYOUT REQUIREMENTS (per slide):
- **Slide 1, cover:** dark background (#0a0a0a), off-white title, red accent (a thin line, a small badge, or one keyword in red). The author's name and handle in the bottom-left in mono.
- **Slides 2 to N-1, body:** light background (#f7f7f8), near-black title at top with a 3px red underline, body text below, a small "[N/Total]" slide indicator top-right in red.
- **Slide N, CTA:** red background (#e90d41), off-white title with the call to action ("Comment X" or "DM the keyword" or "Save and share"), author handle bottom-left in off-white mono.
- All slides: 1080x1350 vertical format, generous margins, perfect alignment.

DESIGN DIRECTION:
- Editorial-style composition, like a magazine spread
- Strong typographic hierarchy, slide titles should be 3-4x the body size
- Minimal but high-contrast
- Each slide must work as a standalone screenshot
- Cohesive visual rhythm slide to slide, the reader should feel the design language is consistent

ICONS & ILLUSTRATIONS:
- Optional: a thin-line custom icon in the top-right of each body slide that reinforces the slide topic
- Icons should feel cohesive, premium, modern, thin-line style
- One consistent illustration style throughout
- Never decorative for its own sake, every icon must mean something

TEXT STYLING:
- Important keywords in bold (never in colour, only the red accent)
- Slide titles always short (max 8 words)
- Body text always short (max 2 lines per slide, this is a carousel not a cheatsheet)
- Excellent readability on mobile

OUTPUT STYLE:
The final carousel should feel like a mix of:
- Apple keynote slides (high contrast, big type)
- Monocle magazine spreads (editorial composition)
- Premium creator carousels on LinkedIn (Justin Welsh, Dickie Bush level polish)
- A Vercel landing page (clean, technical, minimal)

OPTIONAL ENHANCEMENTS:
- Subtle texture overlays on light slides
- Soft shadows under text blocks
- Thin line dividers between sections within a slide
- Premium UI-inspired elements (corner brackets, fine rules, slide-number badges)

FORMAT:
- Vertical, 1080x1350 per slide (LinkedIn-optimal)
- 5-7 slides total (per the input)
- High resolution
- Crisp typography
- Consistent visual rhythm across all slides

## Tips for the best output

1. **Run the prompt as-is the first time.** Canva AI gets the layout right ~70% of the time on the first pass.
2. **If slides feel inconsistent:** ask Canva "make all body slides use the same template, only swap the title and body text."
3. **If the dark cover + CTA feel too heavy:** ask Canva "soften the dark slides to a deep grey, keep the red accents."
4. **If text is too small on mobile:** ask Canva "increase slide title to 80pt and body to 32pt, this is for mobile reading."
5. **Save as a Canva template** the first time. Every future carousel uses the same template, just swap the copy.
6. **For long carousels (7+ slides):** consider splitting into a 2-part series. Engagement drops past slide 7.
